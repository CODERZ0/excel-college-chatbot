5. Admission information







tamil.samayam.com


TNEA counselling code: 2634

The official website provides separate pages for eligibility, admission procedure and scholarships.
Undergraduate admission
For B.E. and B.Tech. programmes, the general Tamil Nadu engineering admission route includes:
- Completion of Class 12 or equivalent.
- Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry requirements as applicable to the programme and current regulations.
- Eligibility based on the prescribed qualifying examination marks.
- Admission through TNEA counselling for applicable government-quota seats.
- Management-quota admission subject to applicable rules and availability.
Postgraduate admission
For M.E. and M.Tech. programmes, eligibility generally involves a relevant undergraduate engineering degree and the applicable admission requirements.

MBA and MCA admissions have their own qualification requirements.
Documents generally required
- Class 10 marksheet.
- Class 12 marksheet.
- Transfer Certificate.
- Community Certificate, if applicable.
- Aadhaar or other accepted identity proof.
- Entrance/counselling allotment order, where applicable.
- Passport-size photographs.
- Undergraduate degree certificates and mark sheets for PG applicants.
This is a general document checklist, not an official exhaustive list. Confirm the current admission notice before advising applicants.
Admission links
